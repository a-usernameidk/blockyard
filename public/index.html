        Rules, privacy and terms

        By using Blockyard, you agree to these. They're short, promise.

        Be a good player

        • Be kind in chat and messages. No bullying, no spam.
        • Never ask for or share personal info (real names, addresses, phone numbers, passwords).
        • No cheating, and no extra accounts to farm coins.
        • Levels, worlds and names have to be okay for everyone.

        Your messages are private

        • Messages between friends are locked (encrypted) in the database. Nobody reads them, not even the admin.
        • If you report someone, only the last few messages before your report are unlocked and shown to the admin, so they can check it.
        • Chat in 3D servers is seen by the players in that server. A report sends the last few things that player said.
        • Only report real problems. Sending false, mean or spam reports can get you a warning.

        Warnings

        Warning 1 → Warning 2 → Warning 3 → Account deleted

        • Only the admin gives warnings, bans and account deletions, and they always have to confirm it first. Automatic checks (like the coin-farming check) only tell the admin to take a look.
        • Nobody gets banned by their internet address, so people sharing a network (like a school) are never blocked because of someone else.
        • You can earn warnings back: every 3 reports you send that turn out to be right takes one away, and so does a level of yours reaching 20 likes and 100 plays.

        The community helps

        • When 10 players report a level, it's hidden until the admin checks it.
        • Players who beat a level vote how hard it is, and the admin makes the final call.

        Open source

        Blockyard's code is shared under the Apache License 2.0. Levels and worlds belong to the players who make them, and publishing one lets everyone on Blockyard play it.
